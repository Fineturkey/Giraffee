# Steal a Monster Egg — prototype

Pitch: steal a valuable egg from a guarded nest, survive the guardian chase, and hatch a creature at your safe base.

Current playable: three egg species, shaking/light-pulsing carried eggs, weight-dependent speed, guardian pursuit after two seconds, server-controlled stamina/sprinting, voluntary abandonment, three-second incubation, creature reveal, coins and carry training. Discover all three species for a one-time 100-coin collection bonus.

World: forest valley, side creeks/bridges, crystals, trail markings, cliffs, ruined gate, egg pedestals, hatchery shelter, horned guardian, afternoon lighting. Scenery stays outside the central chase route. Guardian pursuit has no obstacle pathfinding. The shared hatchery retains each discovered species per player; displays may overlap between players. Progress remains session-only; multi-client testing is still required before release.

Controls: E/proximity prompt to steal/train; hold Shift/L3 or touch Sprint; Q/B or touch Drop to abandon an unsecured egg. HUD shows collection, danger/incubation, coins, upgrades, stamina, and controls.

Playtest questions: Is the objective clear? Is the light egg approachable? Does the heavy egg create an exciting chase? Does training feel useful? Tune src/shared/GameConfig.luau before adding new systems.

Content is original placeholder geometry. No external assets, purchases, or publishing have been performed.
